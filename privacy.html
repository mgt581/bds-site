Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

Bryant Digital Solutions is a trading name of Bryant Group Holdings Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 13671131), registered office 32 Heaton Road, Bournemouth, BH10 5HW. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. Contact us about privacy at info@bryantdigitalsolutions.com or on 07843 969254.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry and audit forms: your name, email address, phone number, business name, website address and anything you write in your message.
  • AI assistant: the messages you type into the chat assistant on our site.
  • Website usage: the pages you visit, the site that referred you, campaign details in the link you used (such as UTM tags and advertising click IDs), and clicks on our phone, WhatsApp, email and enquiry links. We store a one-way hash of your network address rather than the address itself.
  • Clients: the details we need to deliver and invoice our services, such as contact details, project content and payment records.
  • Business outreach: business contact details (such as a company name, work email address and website) gathered from publicly available sources, which we use to contact businesses about our services.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, run free audits and provide quotes: because you have asked us to take these steps before entering into a contract.
  • To deliver services to clients and keep business records: to perform our contract with you and to meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To measure and improve our website and marketing: in our legitimate interests in understanding which pages and campaigns bring in enquiries.
  • To contact businesses about our services: in our legitimate interests in promoting our business. Every message includes a simple way to opt out, and we stop as soon as you ask.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use advertising cookies.

Browser storage and your choices

To measure how visitors use the site, our website stores random visitor and session IDs in your browser's local and session storage. These are not shared with advertisers. You can turn this off at any time using the button below. Your choice is saved in this browser. Our site also respects the Global Privacy Control signal if your browser sends it. Enquiry forms keep working either way.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data for us under contract:

  • GitHub hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare stores enquiries and website analytics, and provides security.
  • Resend sends enquiry emails.
  • Google Cloud (Firebase) runs our audit tool and AI assistant, and Google Fonts serves the site's fonts.
  • OpenAI generates the AI assistant's replies and audit summaries from the information you enter.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 2 years.
  • Client and invoice records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for tax purposes.
  • Website analytics events: up to 2 years.
  • Outreach contact details: until you ask us to stop, or after 2 years without a reply.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it (including for marketing), and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email info@bryantdigitalsolutions.com. We will respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our systems.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
